About this build

FitPortal is the FitPortal sub-team's deliverable for Project Perfect Fit, a Macquarie University COMP4050 client project for Thomax, an Australian logistics software company. This site covers the user-interface / access-layer scope only — FitSolver (optimisation) and FitVisualizer (packing view) are separate sub-teams.
